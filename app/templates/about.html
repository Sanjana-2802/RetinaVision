Project Abstract

"Eye diseases can lead to serious vision problems if they are not detected at an early stage. This project proposes an AI-Based Eye Disease Identification and Health Recommendation System that uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Deep Learning to identify eye diseases from retinal (fundus) images. The system can detect common eye diseases, classify their severity, and use Explainable AI (XAI) to help users understand the prediction. After identifying the disease, the system provides personalized food recommendations, nutritional guidance, eye care and lifestyle suggestions to help improve eye health. For severe cases, it recommends nearby eye specialists and hospitals for immediate medical attention. The system also stores patient history, monitors disease progression by comparing previous reports, and generates downloadable medical reports. By combining disease identification, health recommendations, and patient monitoring in a single platform, the proposed system aims to support early diagnosis, improve eye care, and help users make better healthcare decisions."

Key Objectives Fulfilled:

  • Multi-Class Retinal Diagnostic Engine: Identifies Diabetic Retinopathy, Glaucoma, Cataract, AMD, and Hypertensive Retinopathy.
  • Explainable AI (Grad-CAM): Solves the neural black-box problem by highlighting pathognomonic lesion regions for ophthalmologists.
  • Tailored Health & Nutritional Blueprints: Delivers specific vitamins (Lutein, Zeaxanthin, Omega-3, AREDS-2) and daily meal plans.
  • Longitudinal Progression Tracker: Quantifies biomarker trajectory (improving / stable / deteriorating) over chronological scans.
  • Hospital-Ready PDF Reports: Generates formatted clinical PDFs complete with visuals, metrics, and accreditation.
