Cross-Cohort Zero-Shot Generalizability

Unlike legacy models tuned strictly to a single camera specification, RetinaAI Pro incorporates CLAHE contrast standardization, optical disc caliper normalization (1,500 µm standard disc diameter), and multi-scale attention gates. This eliminates domain shift between desktop fundus units (Topcon, Canon, Zeiss) and portable screening cameras.
